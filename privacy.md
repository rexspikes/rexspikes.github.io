# ATLAS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

ATLAS is a personal assistant built and used only by Rex Spikes. It is not offered to the public.

When Rex signs in with Google, ATLAS asks for access to Google Calendar so it can read his events and add, change or remove events he asks for.

This calendar data stays on Rex's own computer and in his Google account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/rexspikes/rexspikes.github.io
